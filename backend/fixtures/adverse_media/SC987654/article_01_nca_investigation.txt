NCA INVESTIGATION: Edinburgh Consulting Firm Linked to APP Fraud Network

12 May 2026 | Financial Crime Today

Edinburgh-based management consultancy MULE NETWORK CONSULTING LTD — registered at Companies House under number SC987654 — is under investigation by the National Crime Agency (NCA) in connection with an alleged authorised push payment (APP) fraud network believed to have defrauded over 300 victims across the United Kingdom.

According to court documents unsealed on Monday, NCA investigators allege that bank accounts registered to MULE NETWORK CONSULTING LTD were used to receive, aggregate, and rapidly disperse funds transferred by fraud victims who believed they were making legitimate business payments to a supplier or professional services firm. The company's Edinburgh accounts reportedly processed an estimated £1.2 million over a six-month window between October 2025 and April 2026.

Director Victor Supermule, 47, was arrested at Edinburgh Airport on 8 May 2026 and subsequently released on bail pending further investigation. He denies all allegations and has retained criminal defence solicitors at Thornton & Partners LLP to represent him.

A formal referral to the Serious Fraud Office is under active consideration by the Crown Office and Procurator Fiscal Service, sources close to the investigation told Financial Crime Today. The Financial Conduct Authority has also been notified and is considering whether any FCA-regulated activity was conducted without authorisation.

MULE NETWORK CONSULTING LTD could not be reached for comment at the time of publication.
