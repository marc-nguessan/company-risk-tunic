BaFin FINES FRANKFURT FIRM €2.3M FOR SYSTEMATIC AML FAILURES

3 March 2026 | EU Financial Compliance Review

Germany's federal financial supervisory authority BaFin has imposed a €2.3 million administrative fine on Mule Network Consulting GmbH, a Frankfurt-based financial advisory firm, for systematic and repeated failures in its anti-money laundering controls over the period 2022 to 2024.

BaFin's enforcement notice states that Mule Network Consulting GmbH — registered in the Frankfurt commercial register under number HRB 88997, with its principal offices at Kaiserstrasse 19, 60311 Frankfurt am Main, Germany — repeatedly failed to conduct adequate customer due diligence on high-risk clients, failed to file suspicious transaction reports in a timely manner, and maintained deficient record-keeping contrary to Germany's Money Laundering Act (Geldwäschegesetz).

The Frankfurt company, which provides cross-border payment structuring advisory services for European corporate clients and was incorporated in Germany in 2014, is an entirely separate legal entity from any UK-registered companies. BaFin explicitly noted in its enforcement notice that Mule Network Consulting GmbH has no corporate connection to, shareholding in, or operational presence in the United Kingdom, and is not related to any similarly named companies registered at Companies House.

The managing director of Mule Network Consulting GmbH has lodged a formal appeal against the fine with the Frankfurt Administrative Court.
