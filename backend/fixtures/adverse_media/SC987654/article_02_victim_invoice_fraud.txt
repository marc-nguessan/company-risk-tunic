VICTIM: Edinburgh Consultancy Loses £12,500 in Supplier Impersonation Attack

17 January 2026 | Scotsman Business

MULE NETWORK CONSULTING LTD, the Edinburgh-based management consultancy registered at Companies House under number SC987654, has confirmed that it fell victim to a sophisticated invoice fraud in December 2025.

Criminals posing as the firm's long-standing IT infrastructure supplier sent a convincingly formatted email — complete with forged company letterhead and a spoofed sender domain — notifying MULE NETWORK CONSULTING LTD's accounts department of a change in banking details on a pending invoice. The accounts team processed a scheduled payment of £12,500 to the fraudulent account before the deception was identified the following week, when the genuine supplier contacted the firm to enquire about the outstanding payment.

"We were clearly the targets of a sophisticated fraud. Our staff acted in good faith on what appeared to be a routine change notification," said director Victor Supermule. "We have since implemented enhanced verification procedures, including mandatory telephone callbacks to pre-registered supplier numbers before processing any changes to bank account details."

MULE NETWORK CONSULTING LTD reported the incident immediately to Police Scotland and Action Fraud, and has cooperated fully with their ongoing investigation into the perpetrators. The £12,500 loss has been reported to the company's business insurer.

The incident is consistent with a broader pattern of so-called "mandate fraud" or "business email compromise" attacks targeting professional services firms in Scotland. Action Fraud recorded more than 8,400 similar reports in 2025, with total losses exceeding £137 million.
