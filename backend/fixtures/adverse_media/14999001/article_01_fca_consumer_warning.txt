FCA CONSUMER WARNING: Unauthorised Investment Scheme Operating Under NEWCO DIGITAL LTD

19 March 2026 | Financial Conduct Authority — Consumer Warning Notice

The Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) has today issued a formal consumer warning about NEWCO DIGITAL LTD, registered in England and Wales at Companies House under company number 14999001, which has been approaching members of the public with offers of high-yield investment accounts promising guaranteed monthly returns of between 10% and 15%.

The FCA states that NEWCO DIGITAL LTD is not authorised or regulated by the FCA to offer, promote, or facilitate investment products or financial services to retail clients in the United Kingdom. Promoting or selling investment products without FCA authorisation is a criminal offence under section 21 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

According to the FCA, the company — which appears to have been incorporated as recently as March 2026 — has been soliciting investments through social media advertisements, unsolicited telephone calls to retail investors, and referral schemes. It claims to invest client funds in "algorithmic cryptocurrency arbitrage" and "secure offshore government bonds." Both claims are characteristic of advance-fee fraud and Ponzi scheme structures, the FCA said.

The FCA advises consumers:
— Do not invest money with NEWCO DIGITAL LTD or transfer any funds in response to its communications.
— If you have already invested, contact the FCA Consumer Helpline immediately on 0800 111 6768.
— Report any contact from this firm to Action Fraud at www.actionfraud.police.uk (reference case type: investment fraud).

Guaranteed investment returns are not achievable through any legitimate investment product and are a universally recognised hallmark of financial fraud.
